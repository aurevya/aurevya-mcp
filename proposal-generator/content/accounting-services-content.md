**UBO**
Ultimate Beneficial Owner which comprises of any person who directly or indirectly, for his own account and not as nominee for a third party, owns of as effective interest in the shareholding or voting rights of the corporate body.
*(verbatim: "owns of as effective interest")*

### Column 3
**BENEFICIARIES**
Covered in **Section 14** of the *Mauritius Trusts Act 2001*, beneficiaries are persons (individuals or entities) who are entitled to benefit from the trust property or who may be selected to receive benefits under the trust's terms.

**ENFORCER**
As per **Section 21** of the *Mauritius Trusts Act 2001*, an enforcer is appointed specifically for non-charitable purpose trusts to ensure the trustee carries out the trust's objectives. The enforcer must be independent and cannot simultaneously serve as a trustee of the same trust.

**LETTERS OR MEMORANDA OF WISHES**
In **Section 27**, these are non-binding documents in which the settlor expresses preferences or guidance to the trustee regarding the administration of the trust.

**PROTECTOR**
Mentioned in **Section 24** of the *Mauritius Trusts Act 2001*, the protector is someone appointed to advise or supervise the trustee. Depending on the trust instrument, protectors may be granted powers like replacing trustees or modifying governing law.

### Column 4
**SETTLOR**
Defined in **Section 8** of the *Mauritius Trusts Act 2001*, the settlor is the person who establishes a trust by transferring assets to the trustee. The settlor sets the terms of the trust and can be a natural person or a legal entity.

**TRUST INSTRUMENT**
As defined in **Section 6** of the *Mauritius Trusts Act 2001*, the trust instrument is the written document that sets out the terms of the trust, including the powers and duties of the trustee, the rights of beneficiaries, and any conditions or limitations. It serves as the legal foundation of the trust.

**TRUSTEE**
Under **Sections 23 to 33** of the *Mauritius Trusts Act 2001*, a trustee is the individual or corporate entity who legally holds the trust property. The trustee is obligated to administer the trust in accordance with the terms of the trust instrument and the law, primarily for the benefit of the beneficiaries or to fulfill a specified purpose.

> **FLAG for the generator:** columns 3 and 4 are entirely **trust-law terms carried over verbatim from the Trust/Authorised Company deck** (Beneficiaries, Enforcer, Letters or Memoranda of Wishes, Protector, Settlor, Trust Instrument, Trustee). **No trust is being established in this proposal's scope-of-services section** — although the fee tables DO price "Establishment of a Trust" and "Maintenance of a Trust". Columns 1–2 are the standard acronym list.
